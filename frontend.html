Regulatory Quick Reference

DPDP Act 2023

Penalties up to ₹250 Crores for significant data breaches. Requires explicit consent and 48-hour reporting.

MSME Act Section 15 & 16

Payment due within 45 days. Late payments incur mandatory compound interest at 3x RBI bank rate.

Indian Contract Act 1872

Requires valid consideration, lawful object, competent parties, and clear dispute resolution jurisdiction.
